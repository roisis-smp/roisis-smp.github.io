Two ways to play

Same server, same lobby. Switch whenever you like.

Duels

Get matched, pick up your kit, and settle it one on one.

  • 1v1 matches with matched kits
  • Queue straight from the lobby
  • Wins move you up the leaderboard

Survival

A shared world to build in, trade in, and call your own.

  • Claim land so your builds stay yours
  • Player-run trading and shops
  • A long-term world with no planned wipes

Join in three steps

  1. Open Minecraft and go to Multiplayer.
  2. Choose Add Server and paste roisis.mcsh.gg.
  3. Join, then pick Duels or Survival from the lobby.

Server rules

Play fair

No cheating, x-ray, or unfair client mods. Report bugs instead of abusing them.

Respect other players

Keep chat friendly. No harassment, hate speech, or spam.

Survival builds

No griefing or stealing. Claim your land and respect other people's claims.

Staff

Staff decisions are final. If you disagree, talk to a staff member calmly.
